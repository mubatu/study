Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Study is a small study timer for tracking focused study time. This page explains what information Study stores, why, and who can see it.

Information we store

  • Google account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address and name. We use them only to sign you in and keep your history tied to your account.
  • Profile name: the name you choose for your profile.
  • Study activity: the start and end time of each study session, manual time adjustments, and the daily notes you write.
  • Sign-in sessions: a hashed session identifier with its creation and expiry time, so you stay signed in.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in Study users can see your profile name, your study totals for today and this month, whether you are studying right now, and your daily study hours for a month.
  • Your daily notes and your email address are visible only to you.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Cookies

Study sets one essential cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 90 days. Signing out removes it. Google may set its own cookies when you use the Google sign-in button.

Service providers

Study runs on Cloudflare Pages and stores data in Cloudflare D1. Sign-in is provided by Google; its use of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your profile exists. To delete your profile and all of its study history, email us and we will remove it. You can also revoke Study's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account.
